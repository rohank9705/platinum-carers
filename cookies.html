1. Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how Platinum Carers Ltd ("we", "our", or "us") uses cookies and similar tracking technologies when you visit our website. By using our website, you agree to the use of cookies as described in this policy.

2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, as well as to provide reporting information and improve user experience.

3. How We Use Cookies

Platinum Carers Ltd uses cookies for several key purposes to support our digital marketing and local search optimization goals:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for the operation of our website, enabling core functionalities such as secure page navigation and access to secure areas.
  • Analytical / Performance Cookies: Allow us to recognize and count the number of visitors and see how visitors move around our website. This helps us improve how our website works.
  • Targeting / Advertising Cookies: Used to track visitors across websites to display relevant local social media advertisements (e.g., across Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok) and measure the effectiveness of our campaigns targeting family decision-makers in Glasgow.

4. Managing and Disabling Cookies

When you first visit our website, a cookie banner will provide you with the option to accept or decline non-essential cookies. Additionally, you can control and manage cookies through your web browser settings. Please note that removing or blocking cookies can impact your user experience and parts of our website may no longer be fully accessible.

5. Updates to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, legal requirements, or our operational practices. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
